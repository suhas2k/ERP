Dataset: UCI Online Retail historical invoice summary (5,000 records)
Original source: Daqing Chen, Online Retail, UCI Machine Learning Repository
Source page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online-retail
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
Source coverage: 2010-12-01 through 2011-12-09; original dataset has 541,909 transaction lines.

This import-ready CSV is a 5,000-invoice sample selected across the source period. Each invoice amount is calculated by summing the original line quantity multiplied by its unit price. Only complete, positive-value invoices with one known customer ID and one invoice date are included; cancellation invoices, returns/negative lines, and invoices with missing or inconsistent customer/date fields are excluded. Customer labels display the source customer ID because the source has no customer names. Currency is GBP, as specified by the source.

The source contains no payment status or due date. Those fields are intentionally marked Untracked and left blank; no due dates, payment outcomes, or customer names were invented. The full original transaction-line CSV is also available separately, unchanged.
